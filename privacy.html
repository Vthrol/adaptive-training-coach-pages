Adaptive Training Coach

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the local, single-user research prototype.

Project homeTerms of service

Data controller and contact

The operator of the Oura API application is the data controller. Privacy questions can be sent to the contact email registered with the Oura developer application.

Data collected

After explicit OAuth consent, the prototype may retrieve daily sleep, readiness and activity summaries, heart-rate data, workouts, tags and sessions from Oura. It may also retrieve the account owner's activities, sleep, HRV, training-readiness and daily activity data from Garmin Connect.

Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed only to explore the account owner's personal recovery and training patterns and to develop recovery-aware training insights. Access is based on the account owner's explicit consent.

Storage and security

The backend runs locally. Oura OAuth tokens and Garmin session tokens are stored only on the account owner's computer in files excluded from source control and restricted to the local user. Imported health data is not committed to GitHub. There is currently no production database or analytics service.

Sharing and AI use

Data is not sold, used for advertising or shared with unrelated third parties. Oura or Garmin data is not used to train, fine-tune or improve an artificial-intelligence or machine-learning model. Any future external processing requires a new privacy and terms review before it is enabled.

Retention and deletion

Local tokens and imported data are retained only while needed for the personal prototype. The account owner can delete the local token and data directories at any time. Deleting those files removes the prototype's local copies.

Withdrawal and revocation

Oura access can be revoked through the Oura account's authorised-app settings. Garmin access can be ended by deleting the local Garmin token directory and changing or revoking the Garmin session. After withdrawal, the prototype will no longer retrieve new data.

Automated decisions

The prototype does not make legally significant automated decisions. Its outputs are personal research indicators, not medical advice.
