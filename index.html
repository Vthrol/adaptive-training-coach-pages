Private research prototype

Adaptive Training Coach

A personal project exploring how sleep, recovery and training data can support clearer, evidence-aware training decisions.

Privacy policy Terms of service

What the prototype does

With the account owner's explicit permission, the prototype connects Oura recovery data and Garmin training data for a single-user, longitudinal analysis.

It is currently a local development project. There is no public application backend, no advertising and no sale of personal data.

Research before scores

The project keeps provider values separate from raw measurements and tests hypotheses against personal historical data before introducing training rules.

The prototype is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
